Not sure where to begin? This guide will help you create a project and share your first media file.

The first step to hosting video is registering on the platform. The process is simple and takes just a few minutes.

Work in Kinescope begins with the dashboard, which is always pinned to the left side of the screen.

The dashboard has three key sections: “Catalog”, “Live Streams”, and “Analytics”. At the bottom of the panel you’ll find the support chat, integration settings, and the user center. From the user center you can switch to another workspace and configure it, update your personal profile, and view resource usage data and pay invoices.

Kinescope dashboard overview

After signing in, you are taken to the Catalog, where all your projects and media files are located. To get started:

Click the “New project” button in the center of the screen or in the upper-left area.

Give your project a name.

If you need to restrict content downloads, enable encryption for the project. Learn more about configuring DRM in the article DRM file encryption .

Set a default player and specify where media files can be embedded:

Creating a new project in Kinescope

Click “Upload file” and select the desired file from your computer. Or simply drag it into the project area. You can select multiple videos at once for uploading.

Kinescope first uploads the file, then prepares it for playback at multiple quality levels (for example, 1080/720/480/360p). This may take some time. When everything is ready, a notification appears at the bottom of the screen.

Uploading a media file in Kinescope

Your media file is uploaded! Now you need to prepare it for publication.

Preparing a media file for publication

Now that everything is ready for publication, it’s time to share your media file. There are two options: a video link and an embed code.

The simplest option is to share a link. Click the “Link” button in the upper-right area of the dashboard, then click “Copy”.

You can also copy the link from the catalog by clicking the three dots in the row of the media file or by right-clicking on its name.

Copying the video link

Embedding lets you add video to websites. Hover over the row of the desired video, click the three dots → “Embed”. Choose the embed code you need: responsive (adapts to the device screen size) or fixed (preserves set dimensions).

You can now paste the code into your website page or share it. Learn more about embedding in the article Embedding .

Embedding a video from Kinescope

Congratulations! You’ve successfully created your first project, uploaded, embedded a media file, and shared it.

This section covers the main features of the Kinescope platform. You’ll learn what you can do with video, how to organize content, and what tools are available for working with video content.

The dashboard is your quick access point to the platform’s key sections: catalog, live streams, analytics, integrations, support, profile and workspace management. It is pinned on the left and accessible from anywhere in the system.

Learn more about the dashboard in the article Creating your first project and uploading files .

Kinescope dashboard overview

The catalog is a space for organizing your video, where you can group content by projects, folders, and tags. Its interface resembles a familiar file system, making it easy to manage and find what you need.

Learn more about catalog capabilities in the article Organizing your media library in Kinescope .

Kinescope catalog interface

There are several ways to upload files to Kinescope, described in the article Advanced file upload . The platform optimizes content at multiple quality levels and for different devices, and you can watch video immediately after uploading.

The Kinescope player can be tailored to your site design or brand style: change colors, add a logo, subtitles, chapters, and CTA buttons. You can set up multiple player templates and assign the right one to each video. Customization makes viewing more comfortable and increases audience engagement.

Learn how to configure it in the article Player customization .

Player customization in Kinescope

The search bar with filters helps you find what you need quickly. You can search projects, folders, and files by name, tags, upload date, and other parameters.

Find more information about search in the article Catalog search .

Playlists let you group videos into sequential collections. This is useful for courses, product demos, or presentations that require a strict playback order.

Learn more about playlist capabilities in the article Playlists .

Playlists in Kinescope

Deleted files, projects, and folders are moved to the recycle bin, where they are kept for 30 days — this period can be changed. Accidentally deleted content can be easily restored, and you can also empty the bin manually to free up storage space.

Learn more about the recycle bin in the article Recycle bin .

Recycle bin in Kinescope

Kinescope supports uploading additional audio tracks to make your content more accessible to your audience — even when speaking different languages.

Learn how to set up audio tracks in the article Working with files: subtitles, chapters, and more .

Kinescope offers reliable video protection technologies: DRM encryption, domain and password access restrictions, viewer allowlists, watermarks, screen recording and download blocking. Even if an attacker manages to download your content, they won’t be able to play or copy it without consequences.

Learn more about content protection and how to set it up here .

Below we’ll cover the migration options and what to prepare in advance.

To start transferring your media library, write to the support chat within the Kinescope interface and briefly describe your situation — they’ll suggest options and help organize the process.

Audit your media library: determine the number of videos, their formats, metadata, and archive status.

Create a content map: categorize content by topic, priority, and importance.

During migration, you provide credentials with read access: your content remains on the current host and a copy is created in your new Kinescope account.

Full channel migration

For automatic migration of an entire channel, contact the support chat within the Kinescope interface.

We’ll need the channel URL or a list of individual video links. Videos must be publicly accessible at the time of transfer (or accessible via link).

YouTube does not provide a Business API, so the full list of channel videos is retrieved and downloaded in the best available quality.

Some videos may not be transferred due to YouTube restrictions. In that case, a list of untransferred videos will be provided after migration is complete.
